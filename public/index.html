Our story

Bloom began in 2023 with one oven, one mixer and a sourdough starter we still feed every evening.

Ananya trained in pastry in Lyon and came home to Ghaziabad wanting a place that baked the way she had learned: fewer things, made properly, sold the same day. The first customers were neighbours who asked for eggless cakes. We took that seriously, and now every cake on the menu has an eggless version that we test as hard as the original.

We use cultured butter for lamination, Kashmiri saffron, Bronte-style pistachio paste made in-house, and fruit only when it is in season. Nothing on the counter has a preservative in it.

Ananya Sethi, head pastry chef

Questions

Does an eggless cake taste different?

Very little. We rebuilt each eggless recipe from scratch with yoghurt, aquafaba and a little more butter, rather than swapping one ingredient. Most people can't pick which is which in a side-by-side tasting.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Everything is for pickup from the shop. For cakes, a family member or a delivery app rider can collect with your order code.

How long does it keep?

Croissants and pastries are best the same day. Sourdough keeps three to four days in a paper bag. Entremets need the fridge and are best within two days. Cookies keep a week in a sealed tin.

Can you make something without onion, garlic or gelatine?

Nothing we bake contains onion or garlic. Our mousses are set with agar, not gelatine, so they are fully vegetarian.

I have a nut allergy. What is safe?

Each item lists what it contains. We handle nuts in the same kitchen, so we can't promise anything is nut-free. Please tell us when you order and we'll talk you through it.
